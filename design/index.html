A fast, calm email client you drive from the keyboard.

Ten screens for an open-source desktop client for Google Workspace, with several accounts in one inbox. Search is the main screen. Every action shows its shortcut, and every screen has a light and dark theme. Press T on any screen to switch themes.

03SearchMain screen/

You can mix search operators and plain words in one query. The filters it understood appear as chips. Results come back grouped: the best match, then attachments (including text inside PDFs), threads and people. The AI answer is optional and cites the emails it used. The whole index is on your machine: 38 ms across 142,318 messages.
01Unified inboxGI

Three accounts in one list, each with its own color dot, split into tabs. A screener holds new senders until you approve them. Unread mail is bold and has a thin accent bar. Hovering a row shows its actions and their keys.
02Thread↵

Older messages are collapsed and the latest is open. Attachments appear as cards and the reply box stays pinned at the bottom. A side panel shows the sender's local time, your recent threads with them, shared files and upcoming meetings.
04ComposeC

You choose which account to send from, and recipients show as chips. Type ; to insert a saved snippet. Send later suggests a time that fits the recipient's time zone. Undo send lasts 10 seconds, and AI drafting is available but kept low-key.
05Command palette⌘K

⌘K opens a list of every action, with its shortcut beside it, so you pick up the keys as you go. It opens and closes instantly. Behind it is the empty-inbox screen you see when all mail is handled.
06Welcome1 → 2

One-time OAuth setup, then Google sign-in. Your client JSON stays on this Mac and account tokens stay in Keychain. A quiet first launch, with privacy explained where it matters.
07Accounts & sync↵

Three accounts, three clear states: backfilling, incremental sync, and sign-in required. Recent mail is already usable, search coverage grows as history arrives, and you can continue straight to your inbox.
08Settings⌘,

A calm, two-column home for accounts, appearance, privacy, the local search index, and keyboard help. AI is off by default, with a separate toggle for every feature and an explicit choice of provider.
09States & feedbackZ

Inbox zero, no search results, offline access, reauthentication, blocked remote images, and send or archive feedback. Every state tells you what happened and offers a useful next step.
10Keyboard shortcuts?

A familiar cheat sheet over the inbox: move, open, archive, reply, search, and navigate without leaving the keyboard. Key sequences and modifiers are spelled out, with undo close at hand.
Design system: penguin.css (tokens and components) + penguin-screens-2.css · notes in README.md All people, companies and addresses are fictional.
